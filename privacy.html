Privacy policy

Claude Email is a personal tool used only by its developer, Kieran Lynch, on his own Gmail accounts.

  • It accesses Gmail data only for the account that signs in, and only to read, search, label and draft that account's own mail.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • No Gmail data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
